# Ideal Risku Global Limited — Website Redesign Concept

A responsive, multi-page concept prepared by **OPEY DEV HUB** for review only.

## Pages
- Home — image-led hero, overview, service highlights and calls to action
- About — company story framework and proposed values
- Services — proposed service categories
- Products — illustrative product catalogue
- Contact — contact layout and non-functional demo form

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser. Internet access is needed for Google Fonts and remote Unsplash imagery.

## Important review notes
This is an independent concept, not the company's official website. Service categories, product examples, company history, and contact details must be verified and approved by Ideal Risku Global Limited before any official use. The contact form is a front-end demonstration and does not transmit or store enquiries.

## Design and accessibility
Responsive layouts, mobile navigation, reduced-motion support, keyboard-friendly links and controls, scroll-reveal animation, and semantic page landmarks.
